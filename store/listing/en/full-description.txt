Coming off long-term prednisolone takes two to five years. The safe community
method — "Dead Slow and Nearly Stop" — is not "take 1mg less". It is an
eleven-block pattern in which the new dose arrives on progressively closer
days, runs 52 days for a single 1mg step, and deliberately ignores that a week
has seven days in it.

That is very hard to hold on a wall calendar. People lose their place, cut too
fast, and a flare costs them months.

NearlyStop arranges the plan you and your doctor already agreed, and answers
one question each morning: what do I swallow today, and out of which tablets?

WHAT IT DOES

• Shows today's dose, and the tablets it is made from — 6.5mg is one 5mg, one
  1mg and half a 1mg, worked out from the strengths you actually hold.
• Lays the 52-day step out in its eleven blocks, so "what is happening on the
  other four days" has an answer you can see.
• Records the days you took it, with one large button.
• Keeps a hold or a flare without losing the history behind it.
• Shows your progress as a staircase, days on the drug, and a total — never a
  streak, and never a percentage.
• Makes a one-page PDF and a spreadsheet for your next appointment.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

NearlyStop does not decide anything about your medication. It has no drug
database and no interaction checking. Every dose, strength and date is one you
enter, from a plan you agreed with your doctor. The suggested step size is an
editable default that you change on your doctor's instruction.

Always follow your doctor's instructions.

PRIVACY

Your taper is stored only in this app, on this device. There is no account, no
sign-in and no server. On Android the app has no internet permission at all.
Exports leave the app only when you choose to share them.

ACCESSIBILITY

Built for readers in their seventies and eighties, because that is who takes
these drugs. Every screen works at the largest text size your phone offers,
with an extra size control on top of it. High contrast throughout. Nothing is
signalled by colour alone. Full right-to-left support.

LANGUAGES

English, German, Persian (فارسی) and Kurdish Sorani (کوردیی ناوەندی).
