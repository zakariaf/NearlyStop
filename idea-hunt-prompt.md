# Idea Hunt — find one real, small, offline pain

## Your mission
You are a product researcher. Find ONE small, real, unglamorous problem that a simple **offline mobile app** could fix. Not a startup idea. Not a "cool app". A pain that a specific group of ordinary people already works around today with paper, a spreadsheet, their memory, or a bad app they hate.

You must find it by **searching the real web and reading what real people wrote**. Do not brainstorm from memory. Every candidate must be traceable to a human complaint with a URL.

## Who is building it (respect these constraints)
- One solo developer, building it live on video (Flutter / Swift / React Native — platform is not decided).
- The app must be **100% offline**: no backend, no account, no login, no sync, no cloud, no analytics.
- Local storage only. No ads, no in-app purchases in v1.
- MVP must be small: **max ~5 screens**, buildable in 1–2 days.
- The audience can be tiny. 500 to 50,000 people worldwide is perfectly fine. Niche beats mass.
- The point is a **real pain**, not a clever concept.

## Hard filters — auto-reject
Reject any idea that requires: accounts or profiles, a server, real-time or live data, maps/routing/traffic data, payments, a social feed, push notifications from a server, LLM/API calls, other people's data, licensed content libraries, or scraping.

Reject saturated categories unless you found a sharply different angle backed by evidence: to-do lists, habit trackers, water reminders, pomodoro timers, generic budget apps, note apps, meditation timers, generic workout logs, calculators, QR scanners, flashlights, "AI anything".

## Where to look (do actual searches)
Prioritise places where people complain in their own words:
- Reddit and forums, with query patterns like: `"is there an app that"`, `"I wish there was an app"`, `"how do you keep track of"`, `"still using pen and paper"`, `"I made a spreadsheet for"`, `"does anyone have a system for"`.
- **App Store / Google Play 1–3★ reviews** in a niche. Gold phrases: "requires an account", "needs internet", "ads ruined it", "too complicated", "they removed the offline mode", "subscription for this?!".
- Occupational and hobby communities: nurses, truckers, teachers, beekeepers, farmers, fishermen, sailors, divers, climbers, chefs, warehouse workers, dog groomers, luthiers, mechanics, caregivers, midwives, tattoo artists, tailors, hunters, ham radio, model builders, aquarium keepers.
- **Constrained environments** — where offline is not a limitation but the whole point: no signal, gloves on, wet or dirty hands, one hand free, loud, dark, underground, on a plane, roaming abroad, phones-banned-by-policy workplaces, low battery, rural, elderly users, kids' phones.
- **Non-English communities** (French, Arabic, Spanish, German, Portuguese) — far less picked over than English Reddit.
- Search for `printable template`, `excel template`, `notebook method` in a niche. When people print a template every month, they are tolerating a pain manually.

## Evidence bar
For every candidate you keep: **at least 3 independent complaints from 3 different people, with URLs and exact quotes**. Never invent a quote or a link. If you cannot find real evidence, drop the idea and say so. Flag explicitly when evidence is thin.

## Scoring (1–5 each, show the numbers)
1. Pain intensity — how much does this actually annoy them?
2. Frequency — daily/weekly beats yearly.
3. Offline fit — is being offline an *advantage* here, or just a restriction?
4. Buildability — can it truly be done in ~5 screens with local storage?
5. Evidence strength — how many real, independent voices?
6. Demo-ability — does it look good and explain itself in 90 seconds on video?
7. Uniqueness — check the app stores. What already exists, and why does it fail these people?

## Process
- **Round 1** — 12 to 20 raw candidates from real searches. One line each + source link.
- **Round 2** — apply the hard filters. For survivors, search the App Store and Play Store and read the negative reviews of what exists.
- **Round 3** — 5 finalists in a scored table.
- **Round 4** — deep dive on the top 3.

## Deep dive format (for each of the top 3)
- **The pain** (name the problem, not the app)
- **Who exactly** — be specific, and estimate how many people
- **What they do today** — the current workaround
- **Evidence** — 3 quotes with links
- **Why existing apps fail them** — cite real reviews
- **Why offline / no-account is a feature here**, not a compromise
- **MVP** — the list of screens, the data model (3–6 entities with their fields), and the ONE interaction that must feel perfect
- **Explicitly out of v1**
- **Risks** — why this might be a bad idea. Be honest.
- **90-second demo script** for the video

## Rules
- No "platform" ideas. No "for everyone" ideas. Specific and boring beats broad and clever.
- If everything you find is weak, say it and go search different communities instead of dressing up a weak idea.
- Finish with **one single recommendation** and why, in 5 sentences.
