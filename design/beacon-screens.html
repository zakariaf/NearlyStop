Beacon

Beacon is built on one promise: you always know what to do next. Every surface is a chunky bordered block with a hard, unblurred shadow, today's dose is an enormous colour-blocked number you can read across the room, and state is shown as a bordered sticker badge that never relies on colour alone — confident and cheerful, printed rather than glassy.
