Daybreak Design system for NearlyStop

NearlyStop · six screens · 390 × 844

Daybreak

Daybreak treats every dose as one more step toward the morning you are heading for: a warm cream ground, coral-to-amber sunrise gradients, soft diffuse shadows and large friendly radii, so the app feels like light coming up over the horizon rather than a medical chart. The taper is taught by grouping days into blocks — never a seven-column calendar — and progress is drawn as a rising arc, so on day 400 the screen still gives a reason to keep going.
